## Hardware & Workspace
Macbook air m5 - 
  i  am currently using  my macbook air m5 for my college and coding tasks, and let me tell you this thing is actually blazing fast for the workloads i throw at it (currently that is ). i got the 15 inch variant along with 1tb storage and 24 gigs of ram, i feel that this thing is AWESOME! for local llms since due to the unified memory, i get a HUGE amount of ram pool that is the entire 24gb for my local llms. ##Actually it would be 21 gb since system and other apps would take 4gb or more and swap memory would compensate for that. and also it runs fast even without being plugged in the power outlet. and due to it being amazingly light its very easy to carry around. hence its one of the best purchases i made personally i feel.



 Dell G15 511 -
